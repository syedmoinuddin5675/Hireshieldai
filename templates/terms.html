Terms & Conditions

Effective October 31, 2026

1. The service

HireShield AI provides candidate profile, job discovery, application tracking, recruiting workflow and interview-practice tools. Features may change as the service develops. This workspace is not an employment agency and does not guarantee interviews, offers or employment.

2. Accounts and acceptable use

Provide accurate account details, keep your password confidential and notify the platform administrator if you suspect unauthorized access. Use only the workspace role assigned to you. Do not impersonate others, upload unlawful or harmful material, probe the service without authorization, or use another person’s candidate data outside a legitimate hiring purpose.

3. Candidate and recruiter content

You retain responsibility for the resumes, profile details, job descriptions, responses and other information you submit. You authorize the workspace to process that information to provide the features you choose, including matching resumes to roles and sharing applications with the relevant hiring team. Do not submit sensitive personal information that is not needed for recruitment.

4. Automated scores and interview practice

Resume-match scores and practice-interview scores are informational aids, not verified measures of skill or suitability. They can be incomplete or inaccurate and must not be the sole basis for an employment decision. Interview speech input, when enabled, uses browser speech-recognition capabilities; the app submits recognized text and does not record audio.

5. Notifications and security

We may send account registration, sign-in, password-reset and security notifications to the email address on your account when email delivery is configured. No online service can promise absolute security. Administrative actions and security-relevant events are recorded in an append-only audit ledger.

6. Retention and account deletion

You may request permanent account deletion from your profile. This removes your login, profile, resume, applications, assessment results, reset tokens and directly linked interview records. Personal identifiers in audit entries are redacted; a non-identifying deletion event and anonymized audit records may remain to preserve system security and audit integrity. Copies already downloaded by a hiring team or email provider cannot be recalled by this workspace.

7. Availability and changes

The service is provided as available and may be interrupted for maintenance or updates. These terms may be revised; material changes should be presented for acceptance before continued use.

Privacy notice

Account information is used to authenticate you and provide role-appropriate features. Candidate profile and application information is visible to authorized hiring users. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Profile photos and resumes are held by the application for profile and application workflows. Password-reset tokens are stored as hashes and expire after one hour. Contact the platform administrator for privacy questions or requests concerning data that has already been shared.

These product terms are a starting point for this application, not legal advice. Have qualified counsel review them for your organization and jurisdiction before production use.
